# DuoPlan Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

DuoPlan (the "App") values your privacy and complies with applicable data protection laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and how it is stored and deleted.

## 1. Information We Collect

- Account information: email address, password (stored encrypted; the operator cannot see it), account identifier
- Profile information: nickname, chosen color, birthday (optional, solar or lunar)
- Couple information: anniversary name and date (optional)
- Information you enter: events (title, date and time, note, location, reminder settings, attached photos (optional)), ledger entries (income and expense amount, date, description, payment method, installment months), categories
- Contact Us inquiries: category, subject, message, app version, device model, iOS version, app language
- Information created while using the service: couple space identifier, invite code, creation and modification times of records
- Notification information: device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to send push notifications, app language, and whether you receive partner activity notifications
- Purchase information: whether and until when you have a Plus subscription or Remove Ads (Apple transaction ID, product, expiry date, refund status). Payment methods and card details are handled by Apple; the App does not receive them.
- Advertising information (if you have not purchased Remove Ads): to show ads, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (IDFV, and the advertising identifier IDFA only if you allow tracking), IP address, and ad impression and click data.
- Usage and crash information: to keep the App stable, Firebase Crashlytics collects the crash location, device model, iOS and app version, and an account identifier (random ID) when the App crashes, and Google Analytics for Firebase collects app launches, use of key features (such as adding events or ledger entries), and device information. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not collected for this purpose. Firebase App Check uses Apple's device attestation to confirm that requests come from the genuine DuoPlan app.
- Information stored only on your device (never sent to the operator): App Lock passcode (encrypted), display settings (theme, text size, etc.), notification history

The App does not collect location or contacts, does not access your whole photo library, and attaches only the photos you choose to events. Tracking for personalized ads happens only if you allow it. Face ID and Touch ID data are processed only by iOS on your device; the App receives only whether authentication succeeded.

## 2. Purpose of Use

- Sign-up, sign-in, identity verification (email verification), password reset
- Creating a couple space and sharing events and ledger entries between two people
- Showing birthdays and anniversaries and sending reminders
- Sending push notifications when your partner adds an event or ledger entry
- Verifying access to paid products (Plus subscription, Remove Ads), applying them to your couple space, and restoring purchases
- Showing ads (if you have not purchased Remove Ads)
- Crash analysis and usage statistics to improve the service, and blocking abnormal requests
- Reviewing and answering Contact Us inquiries
- Restoring data when you change devices or reinstall the App
- Showing home screen widgets

## 3. Retention and Deletion

- Personal information is kept until you delete your account.
- You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete Account.
- **When you delete your account, your sign-in account (email and authentication data) and all data you created (profile, birthday, events, income and expenses, inquiries) are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.**
- When the last member leaves a couple space, the space and all data in it (including categories and the anniversary) are deleted immediately.
- Records created by your partner belong to your partner and remain available to them.
- The operator does not keep separate backup copies.
- Inquiries are deleted one year after they are answered (or immediately if you delete your account earlier).
- Device push tokens are discarded immediately when you sign out or delete your account, and expired tokens are deleted automatically. Notification history stored on your device is deleted automatically 7 days after it is received.
- Purchase records are kept for restoring purchases and for refund or dispute checks. When you delete your account, the link to your account (account identifier) is removed immediately. If you sign up again with the same Apple Account and use "Restore Purchases", the purchase is linked to the new account.

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties

The App does not sell or provide your personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

## 5. Processors and International Transfer

The App uses the following cloud services to operate.

- Processor: Google LLC (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage for Firebase, Cloud Functions, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase App Check, Google AdMob), Apple Inc. (Apple Push Notification service, App Store in-app purchase)
- Purpose: account authentication, data storage and sync, sending push notifications, showing ads, verifying purchases, crash analysis and usage statistics, app integrity checks
- Countries: Republic of Korea (Seoul region) and countries where Google operates data centers
- Data transferred: all information listed in Section 1
- Timing and method: transmitted over the network while you use the service
- Retention: same as Section 3

When you search for a place, your search text is sent to Apple (Apple Maps) to provide results. If you choose to open a place in a maps app, the place information is passed to the app you choose (Apple Maps, Naver Map, KakaoMap).


Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Before showing ads for the first time, the App asks for iOS "Allow Tracking" permission; if you do not allow it, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. How Google uses ad data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## 6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

- View, edit, and delete your profile and records in the App
- Request that processing stop and your data be deleted by deleting your account
- Request access, correction, or deletion through the contact below

## 7. Security Measures

- All communication is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
- Passwords are hashed and stored by Firebase Authentication.
- Database security rules allow only members of the same couple space to access event and ledger data, and only the author can read an inquiry.
- The App Lock passcode is stored only in this device's Keychain as an encrypted value.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
